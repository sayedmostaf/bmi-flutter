# BMI Flutter App

A BMI calculator app built with **Flutter**. It allows users to calculate their BMI based on height and weight, save the result, and view previous records.

## Features ✨
- Calculate BMI based on height and weight.
- Switch between male and female inputs.
- Save BMI records for future reference.
- View saved BMI records and track progress.

## Conclusion 💭

The **BMI Flutter App** helps you keep track of your health by calculating your BMI, providing insights into your weight, and allowing you to store and monitor your progress over time. Whether you're focusing on fitness or tracking your health journey, this app makes it easy to stay on top of your goals. 🚀✨
